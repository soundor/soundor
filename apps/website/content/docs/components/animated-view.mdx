`Animated.View` is a [`View`](/docs/components/view) whose opacity and
transform animate to each new value. The runtime runs the animation: React
renders the start and the end, never the frames in between.

<Snippet name="animated" height={220} />

- **Transitions:** when `opacity` or `transform` changes, it animates from
  what shows now (even mid-animation) to the new value, with `transition`:
  300 ms linear by default, or `duration`, `delay` and `easing` of your own.
  Other style properties change at once. Its first style applies at once too.
- **Unchanged targets** do not restart anything, and unmounting cancels its
  animations, its children's included.
- **Layout stays:** a transform moves how the view is drawn and hit, not
  where it is laid out.
- **Reduced motion** follows `soundor:ui`'s preference: the browser's media
  query on the web; natively, the host or your code sets it
  (`setReducedMotion()`).

## Springs and paint

`transition={{ type: 'spring', stiffness, damping, mass }}` makes a change
spring to its target. `animation` runs keyframes on their own, here a paint
animation that loops:

<Snippet name="spring" height={220} />

- **Springs** animate numbers: opacity and transforms. Paint (background and
  border colors) needs a timing transition.
- **`animation`** takes keyframes or a composed description (sequences,
  parallel groups, staggers, delays: see [`soundor:ui`](/docs/api/ui)), and
  `transition` gives its playback (`iterations`, `direction`, `fill`). It
  starts on mount, and again only when its contents or options change: a new
  array with the same keyframes does not restart it.
- **Explicit animations win** over the style's transitions while they run.
- **The ref** is the view's `UiNode`, whose `animate()` composes and controls
  animations directly.

The [animation contract](/docs/contribute/engine-reference#advanced-animations)
has the details: timing, cancellation, spring physics, and how the browser
approximates them.
