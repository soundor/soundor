title: ScrollView
description: Content that scrolls by wheel or from code.
---

`ScrollView` clips its content and scrolls it by wheel or trackpad, or from
code: `ref.current.scrollTo({ top })`, or `scrollTop` and `scrollLeft`. It
draws thin scroll indicators.

<Snippet name="scroll-view" height={230} />

- **A view with a style:** a `ScrollView` is a [`View`](/docs/components/view)
  with `overflow: 'scroll'`, and any view with that style scrolls the same, an
  `Animated.View` too ([Overflow](/docs/components/style#overflow)). A
  `ScrollView` always scrolls, whatever `overflow` its style has; turn an axis
  off with `overflowX: 'hidden'` or `overflowY: 'hidden'`.
- **Two boxes:** `style` is the scrolling box, which needs a size (or a
  `flex` that gives it one); `contentContainerStyle` is the box inside that
  holds the children and scrolls.
- **`onScroll`** fires as it scrolls, by any means; read the node's `scrollTop`
  and `scrollLeft`. Scrolled from code, it fires once on the next frame, not
  during the call.
- **To assistive technology** a scroll view is not an element itself; what
  it contains is. Given a label or a role, it is a scroll view.
- **Its indicators** are drawn over the content, taking no space, so nothing
  moves when it starts to overflow. A mouse near the edge shows a track under a
  grown bar: the bar drags, and a press on the track jumps there. That 12 pixel
  strip takes the mouse from what is under it, so leave room with padding in
  `contentContainerStyle` if something important is at that edge.
- **`scrollIndicators={false}`** leaves them out. It scrolls the same; for a
  scrollbar of your own, see [`useScrollbar`](/docs/hooks/use-scrollbar).

## Scrolling from code

The node, from `ref`, scrolls as an element does on the Web:

```tsx
list.current?.scrollTo({ top: 0, behavior: 'smooth' });
list.current?.scrollBy({ top: 100 });
row.current?.scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest', behavior: 'smooth' });
```

- **`scrollTo()` and `scrollBy()`** take `{ top, left, behavior }`, or
  `(left, top)`. They move the axes the style scrolls, clamped to the content,
  and nothing on a node that does not scroll.
- **`behavior: 'smooth'`** takes 300 ms, eased in and out, the same in both
  runtimes, a `scroll` event each frame. A wheel, a scrollbar, or scrolling the
  node again stops it where it is. With reduced motion asked for, it is there at
  once. `'auto'` (the default) and `'instant'` jump.
- **`scrollIntoView()`** scrolls every view around the node that scrolls,
  innermost first, to show it: `block` vertically and `inline` horizontally,
  each `'start'`, `'center'`, `'end'` or `'nearest'` (no more than it takes).
  It defaults to `block: 'start'`, `inline: 'nearest'`; `false` puts it at the
  end. It goes by layout boxes, so a transform does not change where it scrolls
  to.
