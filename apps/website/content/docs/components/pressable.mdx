title: Pressable
description: A view that responds to presses, with its state for styling.

`Pressable` is a view that responds to presses: a click, Enter or Space while
it has focus, or a screen reader's "activate". Its `style` and `children` may
be functions of its state (pressed, hovered, focused), so a button is drawn
from the same state the runtime tracks.

<Snippet name="pressable" height={200} />

- **Presses:** `onPress` after a press; `onPressIn` and `onPressOut` around
  it; `onHoverIn` and `onHoverOut` as the pointer comes and goes, also when
  the pressable moves under a pointer that stays still, or away from it (a
  scroll, a layout change), as in browsers.
- **Long presses:** with `onLongPress`, holding it `delayLongPress` ms (500
  by default) without moving away is a long press, and not also a press.
- **Disabled:** `disabled` makes it neither pressable nor focusable, and tells
  assistive technology so.
- **Focus:** it is focusable, so Tab reaches it; Enter or Space press it.
- **To assistive technology** it is one element, a `button` unless given
  another role, labelled by its text. It offers `activate` (its `onPress`)
  and, with `onLongPress`, `longpress`.
