title: TextInput

`TextInput` is a single-line input that edits itself: typing, selection with
the mouse and Shift, word-wise moves and deletes, Home and End, and copy,
cut and paste through the system clipboard.

<Snippet name="text-input" height={260} />

- **Controlled:** `value` and `onChangeText` keep the text in your state.
  **Uncontrolled:** `defaultValue` gives the initial text, and the input keeps
  it.
- **Events:** `onChangeText` after every edit, with the text;
  `onSubmitEditing` on Enter; `onChange` when the text is committed (Enter,
  or losing focus).
- **Focus:** pressing it focuses it. `ref.current.focus()` does too.
- **Its own editing** is the default action of its events, so a listener's
  `preventDefault()` (on `onKeyDown`, say) takes over that key.
- **To assistive technology** it is a text field with its current text and
  placeholder. It offers `activate` (start editing), `focus` and `setValue`.
