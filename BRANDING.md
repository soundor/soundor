# Branding

This repository is Soundor, the Soundor framework: its CLI, runtimes, engine and libraries, and the soundor.dev website. It is part of the official Soundor
ecosystem. The canonical guidelines for the Soundor name and logos are the
[Soundor Brand Guidelines](https://soundor.dev/brand); this file is a summary.

## Referring to Soundor

You are welcome to:

- say that your project is "Built with Soundor" or "Powered by Soundor";
- write about Soundor in documentation, tutorials, articles and videos;
- use the unmodified logo to link to Soundor or to illustrate something about
  Soundor itself.

## Keeping your project distinct

Please:

- give your project, or your fork, its own name and logo, and mention Soundor
  after it ("Driftwood, a reverb built with Soundor", not "Soundor Driftwood");
- don't present it as an official Soundor release, or imply that Soundor
  endorses or is affiliated with it unless we agreed to it;
- don't use the official logos, or look-alikes, as your product's logo or icon.

License. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/soundor/soundor/issues).
